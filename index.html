Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about AliDrop

Do I need experience in e-commerce to get started?

No, AliDrop is designed so that even beginners can use it. The system provides an intuitive interface and detailed guides to help you start your dropshipping business from scratch. You can gradually master all the features, starting with basic operations.

What expenses will I need to launch a store?

To launch a store with AliDrop, you'll need to pay for the platform subscription (starting at $39/month), hosting for your website (from $3-10 per month), and a domain name (about $10-15 per year). Additional costs may include advertising and marketing, but they're not mandatory at the initial stage. The main advantage of dropshipping is that you don't need to invest in purchasing inventory.

How does the product delivery process work?

When a customer places an order in your store, you receive payment. Then AliDrop automatically places an order with the supplier on AliExpress, specifying your customer's delivery address. The supplier ships the product directly to the buyer. The system also tracks the delivery status and can automatically notify the customer about their order's progress.

How long does it take to set up a store?

Basic store setup with AliDrop takes from several hours to a couple of days, depending on your design and assortment requirements. You can launch a simple store with 20-30 products in one day. More detailed setup, including SEO optimization, content creation, and marketing settings, can take 1-2 weeks for a full launch.

What profit is possible with AliDrop dropshipping?

Potential profit depends on many factors: your chosen niche, markup on products, marketing strategy, etc. On average, sellers set markups from 30% to 300% on AliExpress products. Many beginners achieve monthly profits of $500-1000 in the first months of operation, and successful stores generate from $5000 to $50,000 or more per month.
